Services

Everything electrical, from hitch to tail.

Enclosed or open, pick one piece or the whole system. Every component is designed to work with the rest.

Wiring harnesses

Custom-length main and branch harnesses, colour-coded and loomed, with sealed connections that stand up to road salt, spray and Ontario winters.

  • Main trunk & branch leads
  • Split loom & protective sheathing
  • Heat-shrink sealed splices
  • Built to your drawing or ours

LED lighting kits New

Complete LED packages: stop, turn and tail, clearance, ID bars, side markers and licence lights, pre-matched to the harness.

  • Tail & stop/turn
  • Clearance & ID bar
  • Side markers, front & rear
  • Licence plate lighting

Breakaway safety kits New

Switch, lanyard, battery and charge line, wired into the brake circuit so the trailer brakes itself if it ever separates.

  • Breakaway switch & pin
  • Sealed breakaway battery
  • Charge line from tow vehicle
  • Mounting box & hardware

Plug & play installation

Pre-terminated, labelled and ready to clip together. Designed so your crew installs a full system quickly, without a wiring diagram taped to the wall.

  • 7-way RV blade plug & cord
  • Keyed, labelled connectors
  • Junction box at the tongue
  • Install support by phone

12V battery boxes, charge lines from the tow vehicle, fusing and distribution, so lights and accessories run whether or not the truck is attached.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do you build harnesses for one trailer, or only in volume?

Both. We build one-off custom harnesses and kits as well as repeat production runs for manufacturers and dealers.

Do you wire open trailers too, or just enclosed?

Both. Open utility, landscape, flatdeck and car-hauler trailers get the same sealed harnesses, LED lighting, 7-way plug and breakaway kits as our enclosed builds. Interior lighting and porch lights are specific to enclosed trailers.

What trailer sizes do your complete kits fit?

Kits are matched to the trailer, from small single-axle open and cargo trailers up to 8.5' wide tandem and car-hauler builds. Tell us the length, width and door layout and we'll spec it.

What plug do your kits use at the tongue?

Standard kits come with a 7-way RV blade plug and cord, the most common connector on North American tow vehicles. Other connectors are available on request.

Can you add interior lighting and a battery to my trailer?

Yes. Interior LED strips, porch and load lights, switch panels and 12V battery boxes with charge lines are all part of our lighting and power solutions.

Do you install, or do you just supply the parts?

Our kits are designed for plug-and-play installation by your own team, and we're available by phone to help. Contact us to discuss installation for your build.
